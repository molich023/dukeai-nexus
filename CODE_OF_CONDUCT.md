# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

DukeAI Nexus is an open-source African AI research project.

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project, regardless of background, nationality, language, experience, identity, or technical ability.

We recognise the importance of collaboration, cultural respect, knowledge sharing, and responsible research.

## Expected Behaviour

All community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and consideration.
- Welcome constructive criticism and different perspectives.
- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Give credit to contributors and original creators.
- Respect cultural differences and linguistic diversity.
- Respect copyright, licences, permissions, and attribution.
- Protect private information and confidential material.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Prioritise evidence, transparency, and responsible research.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or degrading language.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Misuse of cultural materials or misrepresentation of communities.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, credentials, or unauthorised data.
- Retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith.

## Cultural Respect and Responsible Knowledge

DukeAI Nexus aims to support African language research and cultural knowledge preservation.

Contributors must recognise that cultural knowledge is not automatically public property.

Sacred, restricted, sensitive, or community-controlled materials must not be collected, published, redistributed, or used for training without appropriate authorisation and consent.

Communities and knowledge holders should receive appropriate attribution and meaningful opportunities to participate in decisions affecting their knowledge.

## Reporting

Concerns about unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available GitHub contact or reporting facilities.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Please avoid public disclosure of sensitive personal information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for applying these standards.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include:

1. A request to correct behaviour.
2. A formal warning.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal of inappropriate content.
5. Suspension or exclusion from project spaces.

Decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and based on the available evidence.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, community spaces, and other activities representing DukeAI Nexus.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in DukeAI Nexus, contributors agree to respect these community standards.
