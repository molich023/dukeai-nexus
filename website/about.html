ABOUT THE PROJECT

Locally accessible knowledge, developed responsibly.

DukeAI Nexus is an offline-first, open-source African AI research project. Its initial technical focus is local access to English and Kiswahili civic and scientific learning materials.

Why this project?

Access to useful knowledge should not always depend on a cloud service or a high-end device. DukeAI Nexus explores local document processing and retrieval, with an emphasis on source traceability, resource awareness, and transparent evaluation.

Development approach

  • Build small, testable components.
  • Prefer local processing where practical.
  • Preserve source and document provenance.
  • Measure performance rather than assume it.
  • Clearly separate working features from future research.

A wider African language and heritage vision

The long-term vision includes research into African language resources, translation, community-reviewed knowledge, and culturally respectful digital preservation. These are future directions, not capabilities currently delivered by the prototype. Language communities and knowledge holders must have meaningful roles in decisions about their materials.
