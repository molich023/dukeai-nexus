WHAT EXISTS TODAY

DukeAI Nexus is being developed in C++17 for Android through Termux and Linux.

HONEST PROJECT STATUS

Research prototype — not yet a general-purpose AI assistant

The current demonstration retrieves existing English and Kiswahili passages. Its demo ranker is untrained and uses a deterministic fallback. Natural-language generation, translation, semantic retrieval, and JEPA are not implemented.

BUILT WITH CARE

Responsible by design

Source provenance, privacy-conscious local processing, copyright awareness, and meaningful community participation are core project principles. Public access to a document does not automatically grant permission to redistribute it or use it for model training.
